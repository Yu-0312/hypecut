# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want HypeCut to be a project where people are judged on their contributions
and treated decently regardless of who they are.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and assume good faith.
* Critique the work, not the person.
* Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution, and that the reason is
  usually scope rather than quality.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption,
and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report problems through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting or by opening
an issue asking a maintainer to make contact. Maintainers may warn, edit or
remove contributions, or ban participants. Reports are handled confidentially.
